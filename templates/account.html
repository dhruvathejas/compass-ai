{% elif section == 'privacy' %}

What Compass AI keeps, who can reach it, and how to take it with you or erase it.

What is stored

  • Your account: username, display name, optional email, and a salted hash of your password (never the password).
  • Your conversations and settings, in a folder only your account can reach.
  • Your devices: a name, type, dates, and a hashed secret. Not hardware identifiers.
  • Your signed-in sessions: browser and OS summary, and when they were last active.
{% if server_mode %}

Where it lives

On this server, not on a cloud service run by Compass. Messages are sent only to the Ollama models this server runs, never to a third-party AI service. Chats are not used to train anything.

Who can see it

Other members can't see your chats. Whoever runs this server administers the machine and could in principle read its files, so don't share anything with it that you wouldn't share with them.

Fair use

This server is free to use, with limits on messages per day so everyone gets a turn. Your remaining messages are shown at the bottom of the chat sidebar.

{% else %}

Where it lives

On the main PC that hosts Compass AI, not on any cloud service run by Compass. Messages are sent only to the Ollama server that PC is configured to use.

What your devices can reach

A device can open your chats and settings once you've signed in on it. It cannot see other people's accounts, and it cannot start programs on the main PC: that works only on the main PC itself.

The owner of the host controls the computer and could in principle read its files, as with any shared computer.
